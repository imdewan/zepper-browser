# Code of Conduct

Zepper is a small project and we'd like it to stay a friendly place to build things together.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Respect people's time: keep discussions on topic, and say so when you can't follow through on something.
- Welcome people of every background, identity and level of experience.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/imdewan/zepper-browser/security/advisories/new) (it's private to the maintainers) or by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful to the community.
